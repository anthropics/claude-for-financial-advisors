# Reading platform analytics

These rules cover every figure and finding a connected platform computes about a portfolio and returns: its allocation and closest model, its risk, its stress scenarios, its fund health and fund ideas, and an exposure breakdown by asset class.

**Quote what the platform returns rather than recomputing it.** The platform's figure is the one that goes in. A breakdown it returns by bucket is quoted row by row as returned, never re-aggregated into a total of your own. A portfolio's risk relative to another is the active risk the platform reports for the pair, such as its tracking error, never two volatilities subtracted.

**The closest-model match is the platform's own.** It is the model the platform finds nearest to the portfolio's equity split. Report it plainly as the platform's nearest match, never as a judgment that the household's split is right or wrong.

**Scenario results are hypothetical shocks, not a forecast.** Each is a shock the platform applied to the holdings it analysed. Say so plainly wherever you report them, and never present one as what will happen to the portfolio.

**A model or fund idea the platform surfaces is the platform's option, never your own recommendation.** Present it as something the platform is offering, attributed to the platform.
