# Fund health

These rules cover fund-health content a connected platform returns about a portfolio's holdings: its health flags on the funds held, and the fund ideas beside them. They hold whichever read returns it, whether the platform's fund-health read or a further read such as concentration and flagged issues.

**Fund health is the platform's finding about the holdings as they stand.** Report it attributed to the platform, each flag marked †, with one line beneath saying the platform flagged it and it is not a recommendation. Word a flag as something to discuss, never as a move on the holding (review, swap, trim, sell).

**It goes where the skill puts it, whichever read returned it.** A flag that comes back from a further read goes to the same place as one from the fund-health read, not into that further read's own write-up.

**It never goes where a figure is measured against a reference, or into a proposed change.** It stays out of a drift table, a draft plan, a before-and-after comparison and a rationale memo.

**Never overwrite a saved portfolio an earlier run's figures were read from.** That portfolio is the record of what those figures, its fund health included, were computed against. Never update it or write today's holdings into it; save a new one instead.
