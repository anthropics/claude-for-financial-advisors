# SEC Compliance Checklist for Client-Facing Content
*Reference for the Claude for Financial Advisors compliance skill. First pass drafted July 2026 — verify against current SEC guidance (sec.gov Marketing Rule FAQ, Division of Examinations Risk Alerts) for high-stakes reviews.*

## 1. Scope Determination

- [ ] Is the sender an SEC-registered RIA, state-registered adviser, or dual registrant (RIA + BD)?
- [ ] Is the communication an **advertisement** under Rule 206(4)-1?
  - Direct or indirect communication to **more than one person** offering advisory services, OR
  - To **one or more persons** if it includes hypothetical performance (unless in response to an unsolicited request or to a private fund investor in a one-on-one), OR
  - Any **compensated testimonial or endorsement** (cash or non-cash)
- [ ] Exclusions: extemporaneous live oral communications; information in regulatory filings; most one-to-one communications without hypothetical performance
- [ ] Even if NOT an advertisement: Section 206 antifraud duties (no untrue statements, no misleading omissions, disclose conflicts) apply to **all** client communications

## 2. Seven General Prohibitions — 206(4)-1(a)

Flag any content that:
1. [ ] Includes an untrue statement of a material fact, or omits a material fact necessary to make the statement not misleading
2. [ ] Includes a material statement of fact the adviser has no reasonable basis to believe it can **substantiate upon SEC demand**
3. [ ] Includes information that would reasonably be likely to cause an **untrue or misleading implication or inference** about a material fact
4. [ ] Discusses **potential benefits** without fair and balanced treatment of **material risks or limitations**
5. [ ] References **specific investment advice** in a manner that is not fair and balanced (cherry-picked winners)
6. [ ] Includes or excludes **performance results**, or presents time periods, in a manner that is not fair and balanced
7. [ ] Is **otherwise materially misleading**

## 3. Performance Advertising

- [ ] **Net performance** shown with at least equal prominence wherever gross is shown, same time period and methodology
- [ ] Time-weighted returns include **1-, 5-, and 10-year** periods (or since inception if shorter), each ending on the most recent practicable date
- [ ] No statement or implication that the SEC has **approved or reviewed** any performance calculation
- [ ] **Related performance**: includes all related portfolios (or meets the carve-out criteria)
- [ ] **Extracted performance**: provides (or offers to provide) results of the **total portfolio** it was extracted from
- [ ] **Hypothetical performance** (backtests, projections, targets): only with (a) policies to ensure relevance to the audience's financial situation and objectives, (b) sufficient information to understand criteria and assumptions, and (c) risks and limitations info. Generally **prohibited for mass audiences**
- [ ] **Predecessor performance**: personnel and accounts sufficiently similar; disclosures included

## 4. Testimonials, Endorsements, Third-Party Ratings

- [ ] Clear and prominent disclosure: is the promoter a **client/investor or not**; is compensation (cash or non-cash) provided; material conflicts of interest
- [ ] Written agreement with compensated promoters (> de minimis $1,000/12mo); promoter not a "bad actor" (disqualification provisions)
- [ ] Third-party ratings: disclose **date and period** of the rating, **identity of the rating provider**, and whether **compensation was paid** in connection with obtaining/using it; adviser has reasonable basis that the survey methodology was fair (structured to make it equally easy to give favorable and unfavorable responses)

## 5. Language Red Flags (any audience, any channel)

Flag these words/patterns for rework:
- "Guaranteed," "assure," "promise," "no risk," "risk-free," "can't lose," "safe" (as absolute)
- "Will" + outcome ("will outperform," "will grow") → prefer "designed to," "seeks to," "historically has"
- "Best," "top," "#1," "leading" without substantiation and source
- "Approved/vetted by the SEC" or use of "RIA" as a credential implying endorsement
- Superlatives about past picks without the fair-and-balanced context
- Fee statements that omit material fee components ("we only charge X" when other costs exist)
- Tax or legal conclusions stated as certainties ("this is tax-free" without qualifiers/scope)
- Urgency/pressure tactics ("act now before it's too late")

## 6. Required/Typical Disclosures Library

Adapt to the firm's approved language:
- **General advisory**: "Advisory services offered through [Firm], an SEC-registered investment adviser. Registration does not imply a certain level of skill or training."
- **Performance**: "Past performance is not a guarantee of future results. All investing involves risk, including possible loss of principal. Performance shown is net of fees unless otherwise stated. [Benchmark] shown for comparison; you cannot invest directly in an index."
- **Forward-looking**: "Projections and forward-looking statements are hypothetical in nature, do not reflect actual results, and are not guarantees of future results."
- **Testimonial**: "This testimonial was provided by a current client [/ non-client]. [No] compensation was provided. [Material conflicts: ...]"
- **Third-party rating**: "[Rating] awarded [date] for [period] by [provider]. [Compensation [was/was not] paid to receive or promote the rating.]"

## 7. Books and Records — Rule 204-2

- [ ] Retain a copy of every advertisement and communications relating to advice given or receipt/disbursement of funds/securities
- [ ] Retention: **5 years** from end of the fiscal year of last use/dissemination, **first 2 years in an easily accessible place** (adviser's office)
- [ ] Retain substantiation backup for material claims of fact; questionnaires/surveys behind ratings; written agreements with promoters
- [ ] **Off-channel communications**: business communications via personal text/WhatsApp/personal email that aren't captured by the firm's archive are a major SEC enforcement focus (multi-billion-dollar sweep across BDs and RIAs). All business comms must occur on approved, archived channels

## 8. Dual Registrant Add-ons (if applicable)

- [ ] **Reg BI** (recommendations to retail customers in a BD capacity): Disclosure, Care, Conflict of Interest, and Compliance obligations; Form CRS delivered
- [ ] **FINRA Rule 2210**: retail communications fair and balanced, no exaggerated/unwarranted/misleading claims, principal approval and filing requirements for certain categories

## 9. Verdict Rubric

- **Fail** — violates a general prohibition or a specific rule requirement as written; must be fixed before sending
- **Flag** — likely fine with a disclosure, qualifier, or minor rewording; show the fix
- **Note** — acceptable, but a best-practice improvement is available
